Controller and contact

The developer of MetriPin and the controller of the personal data received by the developer as described in this policy is Raimonds Arājs, an individual in Latvia. For support and privacy enquiries: rarajs@gmail.com.

MetriPin is Raimonds Arājs’s first independent project. It is being developed for personal needs, unrelated to company work or services. The first version of the app is intended to be free of charge. No decision has been made about future monetisation.

This policy distinguishes three situations: data you work with in the app on your own device; data the developer receives for support or to investigate faults; and data relating to visits to this informational website.

Data on your device

MetriPin helps you add measurement lines and distances to photographs. On your device, the app processes photos you select or take, project and photo names, creation dates, technical record identifiers, line positions and distances. Quick measurements are also saved on your device. No MetriPin account is required.

Projects and photos are stored in the app’s files on your device. MetriPin has no cloud synchronisation service of its own or feature that automatically uploads projects to the developer’s server. Using the app does not in itself give the developer access to your projects.

Photos may include people, private spaces and other personal data. An imported image may retain its metadata, such as the time or location at which it was taken. MetriPin does not promise to remove all metadata.

Camera, photo selection and Bluetooth

The camera is used to take photos when you initiate this action. Photo selection uses the iOS picker, in which you choose which images to make available. Bluetooth is used to find and connect to a measuring device and receive distances. The device name, a device identifier assigned by iOS, signal strength and connection information are used in memory. The distance assigned to a line is saved in the project; the Bluetooth device identifier is not saved in project data.

You can manage camera and Bluetooth permissions in your device’s settings. Denying permission limits the corresponding feature. You can select photos and work without a connected measuring device. A system permission does not in itself constitute consent to send projects to the developer.

Storage, deletion and export

There is no automatic retention limit for projects in the app. You can delete photos and projects in the app. This does not delete originals in the Photos library, shared files or device backups. Files belonging to damaged records may be retained for recovery. If cleanup fails or the app is interrupted, individual or temporary files may remain; immediate deletion of all copies is not guaranteed.

“Export photo” prepares a JPEG with measurement lines and distances. You choose the destination or recipient in the iOS share sheet. The receiving app or service applies its own terms. Export is neither anonymisation nor a full project backup.

iOS, iCloud and the sharing services you choose may transfer data, for example to retrieve photos from iCloud or back up your device. This depends on your settings and services. Backup and restoration of MetriPin data have not been verified and are not guaranteed.

Support emails and attachments you send

When you write to the developer, you provide your email address, any name and other information included in the message, the message content and attached files. These are used to respond to the specific request and investigate the problem described in it. Send only what is necessary; a description of the problem is usually enough to start with. Do not send unnecessary data about other people or sensitive information.

The legal basis for this processing is Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR: the developer’s legitimate interest in providing the technical support you have requested and fixing specific app faults. Processing is limited to the information necessary for the request, rather than advertising or user profiling. You may object to this processing.

Sending data for support is voluntary and is not a prerequisite for working with projects on your phone. Without a reply address or the necessary technical details, it may not be possible to resolve the specific request.

The developer deletes support emails and attachments sent by users within 90 days after the request is closed. This period applies to copies held by the developer, including attachments saved separately. It is not a retention period for users’ projects and does not imply automated deletion.

Gmail is used for correspondence. Google and your chosen email service are involved in email delivery and storage. Google describes its processing of service data in the Google Privacy Policy. This service is not cloud storage for MetriPin projects.

App and Apple diagnostics

MetriPin has no embedded third-party advertising, analytics or automatic error-upload SDKs. The standard Release version does not create the MetriPin Debug file log. An error code relating to cleanup of a temporary export file may be recorded in the system log.

Debug versions used for testing have a technical log containing event times, technical identifiers, operation and error information, without photo content or project names. It persists between launches in two rotating files of up to approximately 1 MB each. “Export diagnostics” lets you choose a recipient; the log is not uploaded automatically. If you send it to the developer for support, the support-data arrangements and retention period described above apply.

Separately, depending on your analytics and diagnostics settings, Apple may make app crash data and usage statistics available to the developer. This is not an upload of the MetriPin Debug log. Apple describes its processing and sharing choices in App Analytics & Privacy.

If the developer receives such Apple data, it is used to analyse app faults and stability. To the extent that it is personal data, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR: the legitimate interest in identifying and fixing technical faults, using only the diagnostics necessary for the analysis, rather than building user profiles. You may object to the developer’s processing. You manage Apple’s sharing settings separately on your device.

The developer deletes separately downloaded copies of Apple diagnostics within 90 days after the analysis is completed. This period applies to copies held by the developer, not to data stored in Apple’s systems. Automated deletion is not promised here.

Website visits and GitHub Pages

This informational website is separate from the app and its projects. The page code contains no analytics, forms, external scripts, cookies of its own or use of browser storage.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. To deliver the page, the service receives technical request information; GitHub states that a visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes even without signing in to GitHub. See the GitHub Pages data-collection explanation.

The purpose of the website delivery chosen by the developer is to make information about the app, support and privacy available. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR — the legitimate interest in providing this information through the necessary processing of web requests, without profiling visits.

GitHub acts as a separate controller when processing data for its own service and security purposes. Its purposes, legal bases and retention criteria are described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The 90-day periods specified in this policy do not apply to GitHub’s logs.

Service providers and international processing

The developer is responsible for using received support and diagnostic copies for the purposes stated here. Google, GitHub and Apple also process data independently for their own service purposes. This policy does not describe them as processors acting on the developer’s behalf in all their activities.

In providing these services, data may be processed outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Google and GitHub publish information about using the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (DPF) for relevant transfers and standard contractual clauses in other applicable cases. For descriptions of safeguards and information on obtaining copies of them, see Google’s data-transfer terms and GitHub’s international data transfers section. Apple describes its transfers in the Apple Privacy Policy.

The developer’s deletion of copies does not determine retention periods for these providers’ independent logs or backup systems.

Your rights

For personal data held by the developer, write to rarajs@gmail.com. Subject to the conditions of the GDPR, you may request access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing. You have the right to object, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interests. Data portability applies where its legal conditions are met; JPEG export alone is not a solution for portability of all data.

The legal basis for processing the data necessary to handle privacy-rights requests is Article 6(1)(c) of the GDPR — compliance with the obligations laid down in the Regulation. A response is normally provided within one month, subject to the extension conditions set out in the Regulation. The 90-day retention periods described above do not extend the deadline for responding to a rights request.

You may lodge a complaint with the Data State Inspectorate of Latvia or another competent supervisory authority. For data processed independently by Google, GitHub or Apple, you may also use the rights-request channels listed in their privacy notices.

The developer does not carry out automated decision-making about you that has legal or similarly significant effects. Changes to the policy are published on this page with the effective date of the relevant version.
